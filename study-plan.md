## Question Paper Creation System

### Who creates them?

**AI + You (validation)**

**Process:**
1. You (or Febin) prompt AI: "Create a 3-chapter exam paper for NIOS 12th Accountancy, chapters 1-3, following the QPD (Question Paper Design) format. Include short answer (2 marks), short answer (4 marks), and long answer (6 marks) questions. Total 40 marks."
2. AI generates the paper
3. You spend 5 min validating it makes sense
4. Print or save as PDF for him

**Paper format to follow (from NIOS QPD):**
- 2-mark questions: 5 questions (10 marks)
- 4-mark questions: 5 questions (20 marks)
- 6-mark questions: 2 questions (12 marks)  
- Total for 3-chapter paper: ~42 marks (scaled)

For full syllabus papers, follow exact NIOS format:
- 80 marks, 3 hours
- Mix of 2, 4, and 6 mark questions
- Internal choice in long answers

**Batch creation tip:** Create a week's worth of papers on Sunday evening. Takes ~30 min with AI assistance.
