SECTION — D (2 × 5 = 10 Marks)
31. Ramesh grows wheat on his farm and sells it to a flour mill. The flour mill processes the wheat into flour and sells it to retailers through a wholesaler. A transport company carries the flour from the mill to the wholesaler’s warehouse.
(i) Identify the type of industry Ramesh is engaged in. What type of industry is the flour mill engaged in?
(ii) Name any two auxiliaries to trade involved in this chain and state the hindrance each removes.
(2)
32. Sunita runs a beauty parlour. She has a diploma in cosmetology and charges fees from her clients. Her friend Kavita works as a receptionist in a hotel and receives a monthly salary.
(i) Is Sunita engaged in business, profession or employment? Give two reasons for your answer.
(ii) What type of economic activity is Kavita engaged in? State one key difference between Kavita’s and Sunita’s activity regarding risk.
(2)
33. A textile company in Gujarat exports cloth to European countries. To protect against losses during sea transport, it takes an insurance policy. It also stores finished goods in a warehouse near the port before shipment.
(i) What type of insurance should the company obtain? Name the principle under which the insurance company will compensate only the actual loss suffered.
(ii) What type of warehouse is most likely being used near the port? State any two functions this warehouse performs.
(2)
34. Due to the introduction of GST (Goods and Services Tax) by the government, a small manufacturer had to change his billing system and adopt new software. At the same time, increasing use of smartphones created a new online market for his products.
(i) Identify the two components of external business environment referred to in the above case. Give a reason for each.
(ii) Which feature of business environment is highlighted by the fact that a change in one factor (GST) led to changes in another (billing software)? Explain briefly.
(2)
35. “Green Earth Ltd.” is a company that manufactures organic food products. (i) Identify any two social objectives and one economic objective being fulfilled by Green Earth Ltd.
(ii) Using SWOT analysis, identify one Strength and one Threat faced by the company from the information given above.
