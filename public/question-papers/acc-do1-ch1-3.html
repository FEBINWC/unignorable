SECTION — A
1. Accounting is the process of:
a) Recording financial transactions only
b) Recording, classifying, summarizing and interpreting financial transactions
c) Preparing balance sheet
d) Calculating profit or loss
 (1)
2. Which of the following is NOT an objective of accounting?
a) To keep a systematic record of business transactions
b) To determine the profit or loss of the business
c) To fix the selling price of goods
d) To ascertain the financial position of the business
 (1)
3. The users of accounting information who are NOT directly involved in running the business are called:
a) Internal users
b) External users
c) Managers
d) Owners
 (1)
4. Which of the following is an internal user of accounting information?
a) Government
b) Creditors
c) Management
d) Banks
 (1)
5. According to the Business Entity Concept:
a) Business and owner are the same
b) Business has a separate existence from its owner
c) All businesses are the same
d) Business cannot own assets
 (1)
6. The concept that requires a business to record transactions at the actual price paid is called:
a) Going Concern Concept
b) Money Measurement Concept
c) Cost Concept
d) Dual Aspect Concept
 (1)
7. "For every debit, there is a corresponding credit" — this statement relates to:
a) Cost Concept
b) Money Measurement Concept
c) Dual Aspect Concept
d) Matching Concept
 (1)
8. The convention of conservatism means:
a) Record all profits whether realized or not
b) Anticipate no profit but provide for all possible losses
c) Ignore losses and record only profits
d) Record transactions only when cash is received
 (1)
9. The accounting convention that requires similar accounting treatment for similar items across periods is called:
a) Convention of Disclosure
b) Convention of Materiality
c) Convention of Consistency
d) Convention of Conservatism
 (1)
10. Which of the following is NOT a branch of accounting?
a) Financial Accounting
b) Cost Accounting
c) Management Accounting
d) Personal Accounting
 (1)

(i) What is the total capital invested by Ravi? (Applying Business Entity Concept)
(ii) What is the profit for the year? (Applying Matching Concept)
(iii) If the market value of the car is now ₹ 3,50,000, at what value should it be shown in the books? Why? (Applying Cost Concept)
(iv) Name the concept that requires Ravi to keep his personal transactions separate from business transactions.
