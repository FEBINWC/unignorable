Passage 1: Read the following unseen passage carefully and answer Questions 23–26.

Trees are among the most valuable gifts of nature. They provide us with oxygen, which is essential for our survival. Without trees, the air we breathe would be filled with harmful gases, making life on Earth impossible. Trees also help in controlling pollution by absorbing carbon dioxide and other toxic gases from the atmosphere.

Apart from purifying the air, trees play a vital role in preventing soil erosion. Their roots hold the soil firmly, especially on slopes and riverbanks, preventing it from being washed away by rain or blown away by wind. Trees also help in maintaining the water cycle by releasing moisture into the atmosphere through a process called transpiration.

Sadly, human beings have been cutting down trees at an alarming rate for construction, furniture, and paper. This large-scale destruction of forests, known as deforestation, has led to serious environmental problems such as global warming, loss of wildlife habitat, and irregular rainfall. It is the responsibility of every citizen to plant more trees and protect the existing ones to ensure a healthy planet for future generations.
23. Why are trees called “the most valuable gifts of nature”? Give two reasons from the passage. (1)
24. How do trees help in preventing soil erosion? (1)
25. What is meant by ‘deforestation’? What are its consequences as mentioned in the passage? (2)
26. Find a word from the passage that means “the release of water vapour from plants.” (1)

Passage 2: Read the following unseen passage carefully and answer Questions 27–28.

In today’s digital age, reading books has become less common among young people. Many prefer to spend their time on social media, watching videos, or playing games on their phones. While technology has its advantages, the habit of reading offers benefits that no screen can replace.

Reading improves vocabulary and language skills. When we read regularly, we come across new words and sentence structures that naturally become part of our communication. Reading also sharpens the mind, improves concentration, and enhances our ability to think critically. Studies have shown that people who read regularly are better at problem-solving and decision-making.

Moreover, reading is one of the best ways to reduce stress. Losing yourself in a good book can calm the mind and lower heart rate, providing a healthy escape from the pressures of daily life. Whether it is fiction, non-fiction, or poetry, every genre has something valuable to offer the reader.
27. Why has reading become less common among young people? How does reading improve language skills? Answer both parts. (2)
28. According to the passage, how does reading help in reducing stress? Also find a word from the passage that means “makes something better or greater in quality.” (2)
