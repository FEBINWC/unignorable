# NIOS Senior Secondary (12th) - Important Details & Guide

## About NIOS

NIOS (National Institute of Open Schooling) is one of the three National Boards of India alongside CBSE and CISCE. The certificate is equivalent to CBSE/ICSE and is accepted by all universities, government jobs, and professional councils.

**Official Website:** https://nios.ac.in
**Student Portal:** https://sdmis.nios.ac.in
**Toll-Free Helpline:** 1800-180-9393

## Exam Pattern

### Marks Distribution

| Component | Weightage |
|-----------|-----------|
| Public Exam (Theory) | 80% |
| TMA (Internal Assessment) | 20% |

**Computer Science (330) is different:**
- Theory paper: 60 marks
- Practical: 40 marks

### Passing Criteria
- Minimum **33% in aggregate** (including TMA)
- Minimum **33% separately in Theory AND Practical**
- Must pass **5 subjects** with at least 1 language (max 2 languages)

### Exam Schedule
- **Twice a year:** March-April and September-October
- **9 attempts** over 5-year validity period
- **On-Demand Exam (ODES)** also available after first public exam (once/month)

---

## TMA (Tutor Marked Assignments) - DO NOT MISS

TMAs carry **20% weightage** and are submitted online only.

### Deadlines

| For Exam | TMA Deadline |
|----------|-------------|
| March-April exam | **31 January** |
| September-October exam | **31 July** |

- Available for download on NIOS website
- Upload through student dashboard on NIOS portal
- Late submission: Rs. 1,500/subject (within 1 month after result)
- **If not submitted, result is based only on public exam (lose 20%)**

---

## Practical Exam (Computer Science 330)

- **5 PCP Practical sessions** at assigned Study Centre (compulsory attendance)
- Block I: practicals in February | Block II: practicals in August
- 50% marks = formative assessment (5 classes) | 50% = summative (last class)
- Must pass Theory and Practical **separately** (min 33% each)

---

## The 2-Year Gap Rule (CRITICAL)

There must be a **2-year gap between passing 10th and receiving the 12th certificate**.

Example: If 10th passed in 2026, 12th certificate earliest in 2028.
He can appear for exams earlier, but certification waits.

## Credit Accumulation & Flexibility

- Don't need to pass all subjects in one sitting
- Credits accumulate over 5 years
- Can add/change subjects during validity (Rs. 940/subject)
- Passed subjects cannot be changed
- **Improvement exams** allowed even after passing (better marks kept)
- Can change study centre (Rs. 520)

---

## Transfer of Credit (TOC)

- Up to **2 subjects** can be transferred from another recognised Board
- Must have passed with 33%+ in last 5 years
- Request at time of admission
- Fee: Rs. 230/subject

---

## On-Demand Examination (ODES)

Available after the first public exam attempt:
- Appear **whenever ready** (max once/month/subject)
- Fee: Rs. 600/subject (theory) + Rs. 250 (if practical)
- All 6 selected subjects are available for ODES
