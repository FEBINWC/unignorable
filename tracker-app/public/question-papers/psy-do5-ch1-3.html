31. Ravi is a psychologist who believes that Meena's fear of dogs can be explained by a traumatic childhood experience buried in her unconscious mind. He suggests that her dreams and free associations may reveal the root cause.
(i) Which school of psychology does Ravi's approach represent? Name its founder.
(ii) Another psychologist, Sita, argues that Meena's fear is simply a learned response that was conditioned through a negative experience with a dog. Which school does Sita's approach represent?
(2)
32. A researcher wants to study whether listening to classical music improves memory. She divides 60 students into two groups of 30 each. Group A listens to classical music while studying for 30 minutes; Group B studies for 30 minutes in silence. Both groups are then given a memory test.
(i) Identify the independent variable and the dependent variable in this experiment.
(ii) Which is the control group and which is the experimental group? Why is a control group necessary?
(2)
